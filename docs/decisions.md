| 17 | 2026-11-23 | The Bayesian model has a common year effect, and the TTC PD integrates it out (Gauss-Hermite). Conservative PD = upper bound of the 90% credible interval. "Pooled" = grade-level pooled default rate across sectors. | Annual default rates are cycle-driven; ignoring the shared year effect would understate uncertainty. The upper credible bound gives a margin-of-conservatism-style PD. |
| 18 | 2026-11-23 | Conformal counterpart: MAPIE CV+ intervals for annual segment default rates from a ridge model on grade, sector and the macro index, fitted on arcsin(√rate) and back-transformed. | Conformal classification sets are uninformative for rare binary defaults. The variance-stabilising transform is monotone, so coverage is preserved and the intervals scale with grade. |
| 19 | 2026-11-23 | Truth recovery is checked by refitting on fresh simulated histories (40 replications in the pipeline, 16 in the unit test) at 50/80/90% levels. | Segments in one history share the cycle draw, so coverage from a single fit is strongly clustered; 96/96 hits in a pilot with 8 replications was not evidence of miscalibration. |
| 20 | 2026-11-23 | Deposit behaviour: account closure times are simulated (Weibull, category-specific shape and scale, balance effect) and fitted with a lifelines Weibull AFT model whose shape also depends on the covariates. Stable share = balance-weighted survival beyond 1 year; core share = min(stable share, BCBS 368 cap). The core run-off follows the conditional exit-time distribution, compressed in time if its average maturity exceeds the cap. | BCBS 368 asks for stable/core separation from observed behaviour, then caps. A shared Weibull shape missed Kaplan–Meier by up to 3 points; category-specific shapes bring it within 1 point. |
| 21 | 2026-11-23 | EVE cash flows carry risk-free coupons (commercial margins excluded); positions are bullets or linear amortisers; floating loans are par at the next reset. NII: one-year horizon, constant balance sheet, positions reprice at the shocked rate of their own tenor, deposits pass on beta·Δr(3M), and floaters' average first reset is half the reset period. | Standard simplifications consistent with BCBS 368 paras 101–107. Betas are assumptions in `config/alm.yaml`. |
| 22 | 2026-11-23 | LCR inflows: linear monthly amortisation of the loan book, 50% inflow rate. Wholesale funding < 1y has maturities spread evenly over 12 months (1/12 matures within 30 days; for NSFR half falls under 6 months). Loans < 1y residual maturity get 50% RSF, otherwise 85% (corporate risk weights > 35% assumed). | The synthetic balance sheet carries one maturity per line, so the spreading has to be assumed. |
| 23 | 2026-11-23 | FTP rate = base rate at the repricing tenor + liquidity premium at the average life (maturity/2), with lp(t) = 90 bp·(1 − e^(−t/3)). NIM after FTP = client rate − FTP rate. | Matched-maturity FTP keeps rate risk in treasury and gives segments a margin net of liquidity cost. The premium curve is an assumption (no market data on the bank's own spreads). |
| 24 | 2026-11-23 | Post-shock floor of 0% (BCBS allows national floors ≤ 0). At current euro rates no shocked tenor goes below zero, so the floor does not bind. | The EU floor (−150 bp at the short end rising to 0%) could not be read; with rates at 2.7–3.7% the choice has no effect. |
| 25 | 2026-11-23 | RAROC is pre-tax with no capital benefit (no return credited on allocated capital): (NIM after FTP + fees − opex − EL) / Euler EC. Hurdle 10%. | Matches the formula in the build instructions; tax and capital benefit would shift all segments uniformly. |
| 26 | 2026-11-23 | The optimiser maximises EVA (risk-adjusted profit − hurdle·EC), which is linear, rather than the RAROC ratio. EC is linearised with the Euler contributions; the optimised book is re-simulated (1M scenarios), and the linear budget is tightened and the LP re-solved until re-simulated EC ≤ today's EC (0.1% tolerance). | Euler contributions are the exact gradient of a degree-1 homogeneous risk measure; the ex-post simulation checks the non-linearity. Maximising EVA at fixed capital also maximises RAROC at fixed capital. |
| 27 | 2026-11-23 | Volume changes are funded from / released into Level 1 cash; LCR net outflows are held constant (loan inflows change negligibly). | Gives linear LCR and NSFR constraints; the real funding mix is a treasury decision outside the segment model. |
| 28 | 2026-11-23 | Limits: risk cover = CET1, no inter-risk diversification; amber ≥ 80% utilisation; red only above 100% (at the limit = amber). The EVE limit uses the BCBS 368 para 88 outlier level; the other limits are internal policy. | A documented, conservative internal limit framework; exactly-at-limit is not a breach. |
